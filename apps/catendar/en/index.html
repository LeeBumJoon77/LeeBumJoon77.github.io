Screenshots

Catendar is a shift schedule calendar and budget diary for people who work rotating shifts. Shift schedule See Day, Evening, Night and Off at a glance, each in its own color. Add and rename shift types however your job needs. Fill a repeating rotation across the month in one go with Pattern Fill. A calendar that fits on one screen Under the calendar you get a summary of the selected day's schedule and money, plus this month's totals. Tap a line to open the full list. Income and expense ledger Pick a category such as Food, Cafe, Transport or Home and the entry saves without typing anything else. Daily income and expenses are totaled by month automatically. Choose won, dollar, yen, yuan, Taiwan dollar or euro. Monthly report Your shifts by type, this month's income, spending and top spending categories on one card you can save and share as an image. Schedules and notes Record appointments, clinic visits and to-dos by date, with a reminder at the time you choose. Your own cat Pick one of eight cats for the home screen and the calendar greeting. A new title is chosen on the 1st of each month based on last month's activity. Share and back up Turn this month's shift table into an image to send to a friend. Export and import a backup file so your records move with you to a new device. Local notifications Reminders for shifts, appointments and notes fire on the device itself and never pass through a server. Seven languages Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish and French, switchable inside the app at any time. No account is needed, and every record stays on your device.
