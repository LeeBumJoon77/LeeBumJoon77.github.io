Catendar: Shift Work Calendar

Screenshots

Catendar is a shift calendar for nurses and anyone on rotating shifts, with a simple budget diary and a cat to keep you company. Open it and your rota is right there. Fill your month in seconds Pick a shift and tap the days. Tap a day again to clear it. For a regular rotation, Fill shift pattern repeats your cycle across the weeks in one go. Your month at a glance Day, Evening, Night and Off each have their own color, with this month's count next to each one, so you always know how many nights are coming. Tap the month title to jump to any month or year. Your day under the calendar The selected day's appointments, notes and spending sit right below the calendar. Tap a line to see everything. Budget diary Log income and spending with one tap on a category like Food, Cafe or Transport. Totals add up by month, and the monthly report puts your shifts, income, spending and top categories on one card you can share. Reminders Set a reminder time for each shift type or appointment. Notifications come straight from your phone, never through a server. Share your rota Send this month's shift table as an image. Your appointments and notes stay private. iPhone and iPad On iPad, the calendar and your day sit side by side. Your cat, your data Pick one of eight cats and earn a new title each month. No account needed. Everything stays on your device, and you can export a backup anytime. Nine languages: English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
