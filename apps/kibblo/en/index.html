Kibblo: Pet Care Log

Screenshots

Kibblo is a pet care log that captures your day of caring for your pet in a single tap. Every meal and every walk fills today's bowl a little more. Log with one tap Meals, water, walks, meds, treats, teeth and litter: tap a slot and it's saved. Log several pets at once, and Kibblo remembers the last amount. You can also log something from 10 minutes ago or any time you pick. Reminders at your times Set a time for breakfast, dinner and walks, turn on the bell, and Kibblo reminds you then. If someone in your family already logged it, it stays quiet. Long-press the reminder and tap Done to log without opening the app. Together with family Everything is kept in iCloud with no sign-in, so it carries over to a new phone. Invite your family to see who fed whom and when, without logging the same thing twice. Health notebook Keep a weight chart, body condition notes, preventive meds and vaccine schedules, and vet visits with costs. Make a one-page report to show at the vet. Birthdays and appointments Get a cheerful reminder on birthdays, the day you met, and every 100 days together. Vet and grooming appointments are reminded the evening before and an hour ahead. Share with people who don't have the app Send vet appointments, a pet-sitting care sheet, and photos from your logs as text or images. Home Screen widgets Log right from the widget and see what care is left today without opening the app. Care for dogs, cats, birds, reptiles, small pets and fish together. Kibblo is available in nine languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Italian. Kibblo shows what you record and does not diagnose or treat. Always ask your vet about your pet's health and medicines.
