TypeDay reads your day from your MBTI together with your star sign and blood type, and gives you something new every morning. Three types, one day The app used to read only your MBTI. Now it adds your star sign and blood type. Put your birthday and blood type in settings and the daily advice gets more detailed. Suggestions that change every day The place, the food and the lucky action come from all three types in turn. Each card shows which type it came from, and it takes about six weeks before anything repeats. A word for today, and your luck One line to get you through the day, plus today's number, colour and hour. My types Your MBTI, star sign and blood type in one place. Read the character, the strengths and what to watch for, and see which types suit you. More A three card tarot for today, this week's flow, and matching with someone. Watch one ad and it stays open all day. Seven days in a row opens everything without ads. Your own dog Pick one of five dogs for your home screen. Collect a stamp every day and your streak grows. Morning reminder and sharing Get today's line as a notification at the time you choose, and turn today's card into an image to send. Seven languages Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish and French, switchable inside the app at any time. No account needed, and every setting stays on your device.
