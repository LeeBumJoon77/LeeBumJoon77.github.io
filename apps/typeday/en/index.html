TypeDay is a daily diary with a little dog that reads your day from your MBTI type, star sign and blood type, with a message, suggestions and lucky picks every morning. A new Home The app opens on a Home where your dog greets you. Today's stamp, the message of the day and doors to tarot and match all fit on one screen without scrolling. Three tabs at the bottom take you to Home, My Type, and Match & Tarot. Don't know your type yet? Take the 12 question quiz on the first screen and get the closest type right away. If it doesn't feel right, you can pick another type yourself. Three types, one day Your MBTI is read together with your star sign and blood type. Add your birthday and blood type in settings and the daily advice gets more detailed. Suggestions that change every day The place, the food and the lucky action come from all three types in turn. Each card shows which type it came from, and it takes about six weeks before anything repeats. A word for today, and your luck One line to get you through the day, plus today's number, colour and hour. My Type Read today by MBTI, star sign and blood type, each in its own group. Then read the character, the strengths and what to watch for, and see which types suit you. Match & Tarot A three card tarot for today, this week's flow, and matching with someone. Watch one ad and it stays open all day. Seven days in a row opens everything without ads. Your own dog Pick one of five dogs for your home screen. Collect a stamp every day and your streak grows. Morning reminder and sharing Turn on tomorrow morning's reminder with one switch on Home and get today's line at the time you choose. Turn today's card into an image to send. The card carries the app link and a QR code. Seven languages Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish and French, switchable inside the app at any time. No account needed, and every setting stays on your device. MBTI is a registered trademark of The Myers-Briggs Company. TypeDay is not affiliated with it.
