Screenshots

Scenevie is a photo diary where the moment lands straight inside a frame. Straight into the frame Pick one of three frames, polaroid, film or kraft, and shoot. You are not importing a photo to decorate later. The moment goes into the frame as you take it. The date stays in the corner like an old film camera, and the place is added only if you allow it. Ten looks Warm film, cool film, sepia, mono and six more. You see the look on screen before you shoot. Leave a line Write the day's line by hand in the space under the photo. Choose from seven handwriting styles and add a mood stamp. The screening Your scenes roll by like a strip of old movie film, with sprocket holes, a projector hum, a gentle wobble and dust. Tap and the film stops, opening the date, the place and the words from that day. Same scene Pin a spot and keep shooting it. The previous shot appears faintly over the camera so you can match the framing, and the collected shots roll by quickly to show the change. Good for a growing plant, a window through the seasons, a child's face. Diary and calendar Find past scenes by date, by calendar or by film roll. Search by place or by the words you left, and see what you shot on this day in past years. Send it on Save a finished scene to Photos or send it to someone who matters. The screening can be saved as a video. Seven languages Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish and French, switchable any time inside the app. Everything stays on your device and is never sent to a server. No sign up, no login.
