Plant a seed. Give it water, nutrients, sunlight and a little music. Six days later it blooms, and only then do you find out what it is. Flosome is a small, calm garden you look after for a minute or two each day. A surprise in every pot Each seed hides one of 48 flowers, and you won't know which until the bud forms. Some are easy to find, a few are very rare. Every bloom also gets its own grade, so even a simple daisy can open with a soft glow or a shimmer of aurora light. Care makes a difference There are four ways to look after a plant: water, nutrients, sunlight and music. The more care it gets, the better its chances of becoming something rare. Worth the wait Seed, sprout, stem, bud, bloom. A flower takes about six days in real time, and checking in once or twice a day is all it asks. Plants that respond Tap a pot and your plant reacts. Hold your finger on it to give it a pat, and it blushes and sends you hearts. A flower album with real flower meanings Every flower you grow goes into your album with its name, meaning, season, origin and a short story. The meanings are the ones people actually know in each language, not a translation of the English. Tools for your garden Drop by each day for a visit gift. Collect 24 pots, watering cans, soils and lamps to make the garden your own, and a few of them nudge your odds of a rare bloom. Nine languages English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Italian. Switch any time in Settings. No pressure There are no banner ads and no pop-up ads. Optional rewarded ads give small boosts, and you can collect every flower without watching a single one. No account needed. Your garden stays on your device.
