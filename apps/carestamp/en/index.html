Carestamp is a daily health notebook for three things people often track together: blood pressure, blood sugar and bowel movements. It looks and feels like the paper booklet your clinic hands out, and every task you finish gets a red stamp. Made for Anyone who checks blood pressure or blood sugar at home and writes it down Anyone who sometimes wonders whether they took the morning pills Family members helping a parent keep track Today Blood pressure, blood sugar and bowel each have a card with that day's readings and medicine. You enter one number at a time, in large print, and see it once more before it is saved. Tap a circle to stamp a medicine or a habit. Finish the whole day and you get a Well done stamp. History Your week is laid out day by day, with medicine and habits shown as a stamp card. Charts and averages cover 7, 30 or 90 days. For your doctor Create a PDF summary with averages and daily entries, or a CSV file. Before an appointment, a guided 7-day blood pressure check walks you through morning and evening readings. Good to know Carestamp does not take measurements. You type the numbers from your own monitor and meter. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or recommend treatment. If something worries you, talk to your doctor or nurse. Medicine is a simple check mark. No drug names or doses are stored. Your records stay on this device. No account needed. The app shows ads. Your health records are never used for ads. Available in English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
