Screenshots

Carestamp is a daily health notebook for blood pressure, blood sugar and bowel movements. It looks like the paper booklet a clinic hands out, and every finished task gets a red confirmation stamp. Who it is for People who measure blood pressure or blood sugar at home and write the numbers down People who lose track of whether they took their morning or evening medicine Family members who help a parent keep their records Today page Three sections, blood pressure, blood sugar and bowel, each with the readings and medicine for the day. Numbers are asked one at a time, then shown once more in large print before saving. For medicine and habits, tap the dotted circle to stamp it. Fill the whole day and you get a Well done stamp. History See a week laid out by day. Medicine and habits appear as a stamp card. Charts and averages for 7, 30 and 90 days. For your doctor Create a document (PDF) with averages and daily records, or a spreadsheet file (CSV). A guided 7-day blood pressure check, morning and evening, before a visit. Good to know This app does not measure blood pressure or blood sugar. You type the numbers shown on your own monitor and meter. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or recommend treatment. If you have symptoms that worry you, talk to a healthcare professional. For medicine, the app only records whether you took it. It does not store drug names or doses. Your records stay on this device. No account is needed. The app shows ads. Your health records are not used for ads.
