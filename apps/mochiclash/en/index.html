Mochi Clash is a 3v3 battler with nine soft little heroes. You choose the team and the matchups, and your heroes handle the fighting. Build your team Pick three of nine heroes: Darkness, Cold, Lightning, Shadow, Holy, Nature, Wind, Fire and Poison. Some pairs work especially well together and unlock a team synergy, such as extra defense or a faster ultimate. Play the matchups The nine attributes form a ring, and each one beats the next. Hit an enemy's weak point and your attack lands harder, with a caption at the top of the screen to let you know. Eight regions, five difficulties Every region has 15 stages on each difficulty, from Easy to Hell. Keep all three heroes standing for 3 stars. Collect enough stars and the next difficulty opens up. Your first 3-star clear of a stage also earns a Raid Coin. Raid bosses Nine giant bosses, one for each attribute. Bring the hero that hits their weakness. You get three raids a day, with 2 Raid Coins for a win and 1 even if you lose. Grow your heroes Open card packs in the Level Shop, raise attribute progress in the Attribute Shop, and trade Raid Coins in the Raid Shop. Easy to come back to Auto Hunt replays a stage for you, Next Stage keeps you moving, and the suggested power shows when you are ready for more. One switch turns all sound on or off. No account and no in-app purchases. Ads are rewarded only and play when you choose. Available in English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Italian. Contact: mall77@naver.com
