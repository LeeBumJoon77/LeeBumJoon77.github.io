Mochi Clash is a 3v3 auto battler starring 9 adorable friends. No complicated controls: pick your party, tap Start Battle, and your friends do the fighting. Light enough for kids and grown-ups to enjoy with one hand. What you can do Build a party Darkness, Cold, Lightning, Shadow, Holy, Nature, Wind, Fire, Poison. Choose 3 of the 9 friends, each with a different attribute. Pair friends who get along and party synergy kicks in. Region stages and stars Each of the 8 regions holds 5 difficulties, from Easy to Hell, across 50 stages. All three survive for 3 stars, two for 2 stars, one for 1 star. Collect 24 stars to unlock the next difficulty. Your first 3-star clear also earns a Raid Coin. Raid bosses Nine giant bosses, each holding one attribute. Bring the friend whose attribute hits the boss's weak spot and the fight gets far easier. The screen also warns you which friend to keep at home. Three attempts a day. Battle scenes Ultimate cut-ins, squishy mochi hit reactions, speech bubbles and a confetti result card. The flow of battle is easy to follow and gets cuter the longer you watch. Three shops Open card packs in the Level Shop to raise your friends' levels, fill attribute progress in the Attribute Shop, and trade Raid Coins for what you need in the Raid Shop. Made to be easy Auto Hunt and Next Stage buttons make repeat runs simple, and the suggested power tells you when a stage is worth trying. One switch turns all sound on or off. Available in Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish and French. Contact: mall77@naver.com
