MarkTrail turns your visits to Korean heritage sites into a travel journal. Open the app and the heritage sites nearest you appear with the distance left. Walk up to one, confirm your visit, and a stamp is pressed into your collection. Every stamp joins its era, from Gojoseon to the modern age. What you can do See the five sites nearest you at a glance, and the ones you can stamp right now light up Browse about 1,300 National Treasures, historic sites, scenic sites and natural monuments by region, as a list or on a map Visit 517 historic sites people actually travel to, such as palaces, mountain fortresses, royal tombs and Confucian academies Read each site, its era, its address and its photo in your own language Confirm a visit by GPS and earn an illustrated stamp Make your stamp passport, one map with a stamp on every place you have been, and share it Follow your progress era by era and climb eight explorer ranks Save the places you want to see and plan your route Seven languages Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish and French. The name, description and address of every site are translated too, not only the interface. Open the app outside Korea and it starts you off with the landmark sites of Seoul. Privacy No account, no sign up. Your visits, saved places and profile stay on your device and are never sent to a server. Location is used on your device only, to find nearby sites and to confirm a visit on the spot. Heritage information comes from the open data of the Korea Heritage Service Heritage Portal.
