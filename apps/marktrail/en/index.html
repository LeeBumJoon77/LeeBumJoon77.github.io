MarkTrail: Korea Travel Stamps

Travel · iPhone · iPad

Screenshots

Collect a stamp at every palace, temple and fortress you visit in Korea, and watch your own heritage passport fill up. Open the app to see the heritage sites closest to you and how far away they are. Walk up to one, confirm your visit, and a stamp lands in your collection, filed under its era from Gojoseon to the modern age. Been to Korea before? MarkTrail can look at the locations saved in your photos, find the heritage sites you have already visited and add them to your passport as dashed seals. Stamp them in person next time to make them official. What you can do See the five nearest sites at a glance, with the ones you can stamp right now highlighted Find past visits in your photos, with every photo kept on your device Browse about 1,300 National Treasures, historic sites, scenic sites and natural monuments by region, as a list or on a map Visit 517 historic sites people actually travel to, from palaces and mountain fortresses to royal tombs and Confucian academies Read about each site, its era, its address and its photo in your own language Confirm a visit by GPS and earn an illustrated stamp Track your collection by era or by province, and climb eight explorer ranks Share your stamp passport, a single map with a stamp on every place you have been Save the places you want to see and plan your route Use it on iPad too, with nearby sites and a map side by side Nine languages Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Italian. The name, description and address of every site are translated too, not only the interface. Open the app outside Korea and it starts you off with the landmark sites of Seoul. Privacy No account and no sign up. Your visits, saved places and profile stay on your device and are never sent to a server. Location and photos are read on your device only, to find nearby sites, confirm a visit and match your past trips. Heritage information comes from the open data of the Korea Heritage Service Heritage Portal.
