Spend five quiet minutes before sleep connecting the stars of the night sky, one by one, into a constellation. Breathe slowly with the starlight The next star brightens for 4 seconds and dims for 4. Tap it gently when it shines brightest, and your breathing naturally slows and settles. There is no rush and no wrong answer. A lullaby that grows with every touch Each star you tap adds a note to a little melody, and the last one rings out in a chord. Calm music, created live by the app, plays softly in the background. 88 constellations and their old stories All 88 constellations recognized by the International Astronomical Union are here. When you finish one, you read its Greek myth, and how people in old Korea and Japan saw the same stars: the Big Dipper, the Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, Subaru and more. Turn it and see real distances Turn a finished constellation with your finger. Every star sits at its real distance from Earth, so stars that look side by side turn out to be hundreds or thousands of light-years apart. Tap any star to see its name and distance. Tonight's constellation Starhush picks a constellation that is actually high in tonight's sky. One a night, and each one is kept in My Sky. Share with friends Share any constellation you love as a beautiful picture card. 7 languages English, 한국어, 日本語, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, Français No account needed, and your constellations stay on your device. Ads appear only once a night when you start, as a small banner in My Sky, and as an optional ad to connect one more. None while you connect stars or gaze at them. Optional nightly reminders tell you which constellation awaits. Starhush is made for relaxation and is not a medical device.
